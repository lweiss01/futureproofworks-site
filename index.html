Prediction markets · Software

Trade Hunter

A live, local-first dashboard for spotting unusual activity in prediction markets, including volume spikes, price moves, and large trades.

The signals are clues to investigate, not predictions or proof of misconduct.

Prediction markets · Open source

PMWatch

An investigative workbench that tracks market anomalies and compares unusual trading patterns with the timing of public news.

It surfaces possible leads. An alert isn't evidence of insider trading by itself.

AI development · Open-source software

Holistic

AI agents and coding tools come and go. Holistic helps the project remember decisions, checkpoints, handoffs, and things that shouldn't break again.

An npm package with human-reviewed checkpoints and optional cross-device continuity.

The thread that connects it

What should survive when everything else changes?

That's the question behind a surprising amount of this work. Projects need continuity, research needs provenance, and new ideas should be able to grow without erasing what came before.

How I approach it: Ask a question. Investigate it. Build something to test the idea. Publish what happened, including the parts that didn't work.
