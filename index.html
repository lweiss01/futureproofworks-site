About Future Proof Works

Built at the intersection.

Future Proof Works is the independent studio of Lisa Fremlin Weiss, a systems thinker, analyst, writer, and builder whose work explores how complex systems preserve continuity while participants and conditions change.

The studio spans software, research, publishing, and hardware experiments. Different domains. The same underlying question.

How do we help the future understand what matters now?
