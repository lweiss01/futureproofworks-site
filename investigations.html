Trade Hunter

A live, local-first dashboard for spotting unusual activity in prediction markets, including volume spikes, price moves, and large trades.

The signals are clues to investigate, not predictions or proof of misconduct.

Prediction markets · Open source

PMWatch

PMWatch looks for prediction-market patterns that could point to insider trading involving government employees or others with access to nonpublic government information. It compares unusual trading activity with the timing of public announcements and news.

Public market data cannot identify individual traders. Its alerts are investigative leads, not proof of insider trading.
