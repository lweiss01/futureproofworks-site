Trade Hunter

A live, local-first dashboard for spotting unusual activity in prediction markets, including volume spikes, price moves, and large trades.

Prediction markets · Open source

PMWatch

An investigative workbench that tracks market anomalies and compares unusual trading patterns with the timing of public news.

It surfaces possible leads. An alert isn't evidence of insider trading by itself.
