The workshop is where I try ideas out in the real world. Some experiments become finished objects. Others get revised, set aside, or teach me that I was asking the wrong question.

Physical computing

Complete enough to leave the workshop.

Not every computer should look or behave like a computer. Future Proof Works explores clocks, lights, displays, sensors, and quiet objects that help people orient themselves without competing for attention.

These projects bring together software, electronics, fabrication, and interaction design. Getting a circuit to work is one step. Making something useful, understandable, and at home in its surroundings is another.

PHYSICAL COMPUTING · COMPLETED

Ambient color display

Model demonstrated: A small computational object can shape the atmosphere of a room without demanding attention.

A custom-enclosed touchscreen display that generates a new random color every three minutes, calculates readable contrasting text, and fades smoothly from one color to the next. Hidden touch controls close the application or advance immediately to another color.

PUBLISHING SYSTEM · IN DEVELOPMENT · NOT RELEASED

Saga Publication System

What I'm testing: Can one publishing system preserve methodology, history, evidence, and supersession while supporting different kinds of work, from websites and books to software releases, technical reports, and recurring publications?

Saga brings together structured publication data, continuity-aware logic, and a deterministic Typst renderer. The Future Proof Works website is an early test case. The system is still in development and is not available as a public release.
