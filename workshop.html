CURRENT · EXPERIMENTS AND BUILDS

Where models meet reality.

The workshop holds experiments being built, tested, revised, or discarded, along with finished objects that have earned their way out of the workshop. This is where an investigation becomes concrete.

Physical computing

Complete enough to leave the workshop.

Not every computer should look or behave like a computer. Future Proof Works explores clocks, lights, displays, sensors, and quiet objects that help people orient themselves without competing for attention.

These builds combine software, electronics, fabrication, and interaction design. A working circuit is a prototype. A finished object is something that can belong in the place where it lives.

Ambient color display

Model demonstrated: A small computational object can shape the atmosphere of a room without demanding attention.

A custom-enclosed touchscreen display that generates a new random color every three minutes, calculates readable contrasting text, and fades smoothly from one color to the next. Hidden touch controls close the application or advance immediately to another color.
