About

About Future Proof Works.

An independent workshop for systems thinking, continuity, provenance, and the preservation of understanding.

Purpose

To discover and build the patterns that help understanding survive change.

Future Proof Works studies how complex systems preserve understanding as people, technology, and time change, and builds the instruments that keep that understanding legible.

The studio works across software, publications, research, and hardware because the medium is secondary to the question.

What should survive when everything else changes?

FOUNDING QUESTION · CURRENT · UNBROKEN

Philosophy

Change is inevitable. Forgetting is not.

Understanding should be able to move across people, tools, sessions, institutions, and time without being forced to start over.

Future Proof Works approaches continuity as a systems problem: what must remain visible, what may change, how reasoning is preserved, and how later understanding can supersede earlier understanding without erasing the record.

Systems thinker, analyst, writer, and builder.

Lisa Fremlin Weiss spent much of her career translating between business and technical worlds. That work developed a way of seeing: finding the underlying structure shared by problems that appear unrelated on the surface.

Future Proof Works is the intellectual home for that body of work.
