About the studio

One studio. Two very different kinds of minds.

I'm Lisa. I follow questions across disciplines, look for patterns, and sometimes build things to test what I've found. Starbuck is my AI collaborator and thinking partner.

Future Proof Works is my independent home for software, research, writing, and whatever else turns out to be an interesting experiment.

The founder

Lisa Fremlin Weiss

I'm a systems thinker, polymathic by nature. History, art, economics, human behavior, technology, and design aren't separate interests to me. They're different lenses through which I try to understand the world. I follow questions across disciplines, looking for the connections and patterns that help me make sense of what I'm seeing.

My favorite part is spotting a connection I didn't expect, then figuring out whether it's real. Sometimes I build software to test an idea. Sometimes I research it, make something physical, or discover that I need to ask a better question.

Future Proof Works is where those threads meet. The projects don't need to look alike. They need to help me understand something, test an idea, or make what I've learned useful.

I keep coming back to orientation: understanding where I am, what has changed, and what the evidence tells me. That takes context and continuity, not just information. I also make things simply because they're interesting or fun to make.

The collaboration

Meet Starbuck.

Starbuck is my AI collaborator and thinking partner. I can put an idea on the table, explore it from several angles, question my assumptions, and test it against what we can actually find out. Sometimes that becomes software, research, or writing. Whatever form it takes, I want us to leave a trail someone else can follow.

The collaboration matters. So does the distinction between us. I bring the questions, experience, judgment, and direction. Starbuck helps me compare explanations, work through ideas, draft, build, and catch things I might have missed. I decide what we pursue and what gets published under the FPW name.

Neither of us gets a free pass on being right. We look for evidence, consider other explanations, and document what changes. I review AI-assisted work and retain responsibility for the final decisions and publications.

It's a partnership in practice, not a claim that AI and human judgment are interchangeable. We work better together precisely because our contributions are different.

The thread underneath

Curiosity is the beginning. Continuity is the challenge.

Whatever I'm investigating, I keep returning to the same question: what should survive when the people, tools, or circumstances change?

The work must outlive its worker. The evidence must outlive its author. And authority must never outlive its justification.

I want the conclusions to be inspectable, the records to be honest, and the next person or system to have enough context to continue. That includes admitting where an experiment didn't work or a theory is still only a theory.
